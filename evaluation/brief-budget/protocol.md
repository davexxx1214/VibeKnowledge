short atomic authoring guidance. Keep facts, evidence, schema, discovery,
freshness rules, task context and public CLI/MCP parameters unchanged. Do not
regenerate historical briefs or reinstate ranking/session/filter experiments.

all eight available cards in three retained snapshots: two VibeKnowledge cards,
five Yuxi frontend cards, and one Shelf synthetic card. All are reused development
inputs, not held-out data; Yuxi is only source material, not a reused algorithm.
